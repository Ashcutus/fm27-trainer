## FM27 Trainer 0.2.7 — Tactics page, and the Mac app finds FM again

### New
- **Tactics page.** The app reads your tactic from FM: the eleven places with their role in and out of
  possession, and each player's positions and attributes. Per place you see how well the player fits, whether
  someone outside the eleven fits better, the best eleven for the tactic, and roles in the same line that suit a
  player better. You can look at all three of your tactics. It only reads: nothing is changed in FM.
  - Which attributes belong to a role is our own estimate, not a number from FM: treat the score as a guide.
  - Left and right are derived from the order of the places.
  - A few roles are not named yet; they show as a number (for example `#41`).
  - Checked live on the Mac (Steam). On Windows it has only been tested in simulation: please report what you see.

### Fixed
- **Mac: "No Football Manager window found" while FM was running.** With FM full screen on its own Space, the app
  could not see the window from another Space. It now finds and reads the FM window wherever it is, so auto-read
  also works while you are looking at the app. Without Screen Recording permission the app now says so.
